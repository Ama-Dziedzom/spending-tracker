3. What LogIt does and doesn’t do

LogIt reads the money alerts your providers send you, turns them into a list of transactions, and shows totals, insights and limits. It is not a bank, a mobile money provider, a payment service or a lender. It can’t move money, see your provider account directly, or change anything with your provider.

LogIt is not connected to or endorsed by MTN, Telecel, AT, or any bank whose alerts it reads. Their names are used only to describe which alerts LogIt understands.

4. Accuracy

LogIt works from the text of each alert, so it can only be as accurate as the alerts it receives. It can miss a message, misread one, or estimate a date or a balance. Alerts it isn’t sure about go to Check for you to confirm.

Always rely on your provider’s own app, statement or USSD balance for anything that matters, such as making a payment, settling a dispute or filing taxes. If something looks wrong in LogIt, you can correct it in the app.
