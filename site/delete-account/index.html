Delete your LogIt account

You can delete your account and all your LogIt data at any time, with or without the app.

In the app

  1. Open LogIt and tap your avatar at the top of Spending to open Settings.
  2. Tap Profile, then Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

This can’t be undone. If you want a copy of your transactions, export them first: Settings › Help & privacy › Export my transactions.

Without the app

  1. Email [CONTACT EMAIL] with the subject Delete my LogIt account.
  2. Include the phone number you sign in to LogIt with.
  3. To protect your account, I’ll text a code to that number. Reply with the code.
  4. I’ll delete the account and email you when it’s done, within 7 days.

You can also send the same request on WhatsApp: [WHATSAPP NUMBER].

What is deleted

Everything linked to your account is permanently removed:

  • your account, name, phone number and email
  • all transactions and the alert text kept with them
  • wallets, transfers and balances
  • alerts waiting in Check, and senders LogIt learned
  • weekly limits, expected income and category changes
  • masked templates from “Help LogIt read more banks”
  • your settings and Ask LogIt question history

What may be kept for a while

  • Server logs: up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Database backups: [up to 7 days / none], then overwritten.
  • Ask LogIt chat answers (if you used them): Anthropic deletes what it received within 30 days.
  • MTN transaction ID and time list: kept, because it holds no name, number, account or amount and can’t be linked to you.

None of these is used for anything else. Read the full Privacy Policy.
